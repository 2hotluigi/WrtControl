# Code of Conduct

WrtControl adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or language
(English and Spanish are both first-class here). Criticize ideas and code, not people. No
harassment, insults, personal attacks or publishing someone's private information, including the
network details that show up in router reports.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior in this project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) breaks these
rules, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or the private security form in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues and may block people who break these rules.
